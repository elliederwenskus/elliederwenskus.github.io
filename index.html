About

Ellie Derwenskus is an architectural designer and project manager based in New York, NY. B.Arch, Syracuse University School of Architecture (2018–2023). This site presents her selected-works portfolio from those years — thesis, studio, internship and elective work.

Her framing of the work: a building is a product — shaped by client input, test fits, prototypes and trial-and-error iteration, from first sketch to fabrication.
